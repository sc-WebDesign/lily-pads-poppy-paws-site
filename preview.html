Nervous dogs welcome

The salon for dogs who’ve been turned away

Some dogs find grooming genuinely scary, and plenty of salons will not take them. We are the opposite. One of our regulars, a deaf fox terrier, had been refused by groomer after groomer before finding us; his owner wrote about it in our reviews, and he now gets his classic terrier groom here happily.

  • ✓ Extra time built in. Anxious dogs are never rushed through someone else’s schedule.
  • ✓ Quiet, one-to-one handling. A small salon means no wall of barking strangers.
  • ✓ Meet-us visits welcome. Pop in, have a sniff around, no clippers in sight.
