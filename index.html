Meet the Team

Grooming with real patience, not a production line.

Lily Pads & Poppy Paws is a small, independent grooming salon on Lomond Road. It's the kind of place where your dog is a name, not a number. Led by groomer Lucy and a friendly, dog-obsessed team, every appointment is booked one-on-one, so your dog gets a calm, unrushed groom instead of a rushed slot on a conveyor belt.

We've built a reputation for taking real time and care with nervous, reactive, senior and rescue dogs, the ones other salons sometimes struggle with. If your dog needs a little extra patience, that's exactly what you'll get here.

  • ✓ Small, independent salon, not a chain or a franchise.
  • ✓ Specialist patience with nervous, senior and rescue dogs.
  • ✓ Loyal local following: customers who've moved away still travel back.
